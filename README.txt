JESRYL COMELING PORTFOLIO
=========================

Open index.html in your browser (double-click it).

FOLDERS
  index.html     all the text and sections (name, about, experience, projects, contact)
  css/style.css  colors, fonts, layout, animations
  js/main.js     animations; the SETTINGS block at the top is the easy part to edit
  images/        put your pictures here

WHERE TO PUT YOUR PICTURE (the entrance)
  1. images/hero.png      big picture in the entrance, in front of the JESRYL letters.
                          Use a PNG with a transparent background (cutout of you, waist up).
  2. images/profile.jpg   picture in the "Hi, I'm Jesryl!" section (any JPG, portrait shape).

  Keep these exact file names, or change the src="..." on the <img> lines in index.html
  (search for "hero.png" and "profile.jpg"). If a file is missing, the page hides it
  and the design still looks fine.
